### Question H31

#### 1. Conversion of Decadic Slope to Natural Logarithm
The measured slope is decadic: $\frac{d\gamma}{d\log_{10} C} = -9.2\text{ mN/m per decade}$.
Converting to natural logarithm:
$$\frac{d\gamma}{d\ln C} = \frac{1}{\ln(10)} \frac{d\gamma}{d\log_{10} C} = \frac{-9.2}{2.302585} = -3.99551\text{ mN/m}$$

#### 2. Gibbs Adsorption Isotherm
- **Prefactor $n$ used**: **$n = 2$**. Because this is a 1:1 ionic surfactant in water with **NO added electrolyte**, both surfactant ions contribute equally to chemical potential changes ($d\mu = 2 R T d\ln C$).
- **Maximum surface excess ($\Gamma_\text{max}$)** via `gibbs_surface_excess`:
  $$\Gamma_\text{max} = -\frac{1}{2 R T} \frac{d\gamma}{d\ln C} = -\frac{-3.99551 \times 10^{-3}}{2 \times 8.314462618 \times 298.15} = 8.05885 \times 10^{-7}\text{ mol/m}^2$$
- **Minimum area per molecule ($A_\text{min}$)** via `gibbs_area_per_molecule`:
  $$A_\text{min} = \frac{1}{\Gamma_\text{max} N_A} = \frac{1}{8.05885 \times 10^{-7} \times 6.02214 \times 10^{23}} \times 10^{18} = 2.06052\text{ nm}^2$$

#### 3. Standard Free Energy of Micellization
With $\text{CMC} = 6.0\text{ mM} = 0.006\text{ M}$, $\alpha = 0.30$, counterion factor $2 - \alpha = 1.70$:
$$\Delta G^\circ_\text{mic} = (2 - \alpha) R T \ln\left(\frac{\text{CMC}}{55.5}\right) = 1.70 \times 8.314462618 \times 298.15 \times \ln\left(\frac{0.006}{55.5}\right) \times 10^{-3} = -38.4857\text{ kJ/mol}$$
*(via `gibbs_free_energy_micellization`: $-38.4864\text{ kJ/mol}$)*.
