Audit of CPC + TX‑114 / Reactive Red 2 thermodynamics
(a) ΔG at 293.15 K from eq (5)

Using X_cmc = (0.87×10⁻³ mol/L)/55.5 = 1.5676×10⁻⁵, ln X_cmc = −11.0634, (2 − α) = 1.516, RT = 2.4374 kJ/mol:

ΔG = (1.516)(2.4374)(−11.0634) = −40.9 kJ/mol (−40.88)

The printed Table‑4 value at 293.15 K is −32.4 kJ/mol, which does not follow from eq (5) with the printed CMC and α. (The other three printed ΔG values do check out: eq (5) gives −41.6, −47.8, −52.0 vs printed −41.5, −47.6, −51.9.)

Comparison to CPC alone (−36.1 kJ/mol): the correctly computed −40.9 is more negative than −36.1, so the “more spontaneous than CPC alone” claim actually holds for the correct number. Note the irony — the paper’s own printed −32.4 is less negative than −36.1 and would contradict that very conclusion.

(b) Enthalpy from the ΔG(T) series

The only enthalpy route from a ΔG(T) series is ΔS = −d(ΔG)/dT, ΔH = ΔG + TΔS. Using backward differences on the eq‑(5) series (−40.88, −41.60, −47.80, −52.05), which is why only the three later temperatures are obtainable:

T (K)	ΔS = −ΔΔG/ΔT (kJ/mol·K)	ΔH = ΔG + TΔS (kJ/mol)
303.15	+0.072	−19.7
313.15	+0.620	+146.4
323.15	+0.424	+85.1

These bear no resemblance to the printed ΔH (−5.2, −6.2, −7.1 kJ/mol). The erratic α values (0.484, 0.505, 0.332, 0.230) make (2−α) swing, so ΔG(T) is non‑smooth; the implied entropies (72–620 J/mol·K) are physically unreasonable and two of the three enthalpies come out strongly endothermic (positive).

Does the printed result follow?

No.

The printed ΔG(293.15) = −32.4 is arithmetically wrong (eq (5) gives −40.9).
The printed smooth, exothermic ΔH (−5.0 to −7.1) cannot be derived from the ΔG(T) series — the series yields −19.7, +146.4, +85.1 kJ/mol, mostly endothermic. The printed ΔS/ΔH pairs are internally consistent with each other (ΔG = ΔH − TΔS) but are not the derivative of the stated ΔG(T) data.
The “exothermic” conclusion is unsupported by these data; the “more spontaneous than CPC alone” conclusion happens to be true only after correcting the ΔG arithmetic.
