Method

eq (5): ΔG = (2 − α)·R·T·ln(X_cmc), with X_cmc = (CMC in mol/L)/55.5.

Enthalpy from the ΔG(T) series via ΔS = −d(ΔG)/dT (finite differences) and ΔH = ΔG + T·ΔS. Interior temperatures use a central difference; the endpoint (323.15 K) uses a backward difference.

(a) ΔG at 293.15 K

CMC = 0.87 mM → X_cmc = 8.70×10⁻⁴/55.5 = 1.5676×10⁻⁵, ln X_cmc = −11.0634
RT = 8.314462618×10⁻³ × 293.15 = 2.43738 kJ/mol; (2 − α) = 1.516

ΔG = 1.516 × 2.43738 × (−11.0634) = −40.88 kJ/mol

(For reference, the full series: −40.880, −41.603, −47.805, −52.048 kJ/mol at 293.15/303.15/313.15/323.15 K.)
