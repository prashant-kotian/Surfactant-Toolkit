================================================================================
Nonionic surfactant, 298.15 K, gamma_0 = 72.0 mN/m.

Gibbs surface excess from local slope: Gamma = -(1/RT) d gamma/d ln c.
-d gamma/d ln c (~ RT*Gamma, mN/m) is already large at the lowest c and only
creeps to a plateau:
  0.05-0.10 mM : 6.96   (theta ~ 0.94)
  0.20-0.40 mM : 7.34   (theta ~ 0.99)
  1.30-1.90 mM : 7.42   (theta ~ 1.00)
=> interface essentially saturated (theta ~ 0.93-1.0) across the whole pre-CMC
range; Gamma_max ~ 3.0e-6 mol/m^2 (~55 A^2/molecule).

Diagnostic: at c = 0.05 mM coverage is ~0.93 but pi is only 5.19 mN/m, whereas
Langmuir/Szyszkowski pi = -RT*Gamma_max*ln(1-theta) demands ~20 mN/m. A single
Langmuir isotherm cannot fit both the low-c pressure and the near-constant slope.
The Frumkin equation of state pi = -RT*Gamma_max[ln(1-theta) + beta*theta^2] fits
self-consistently with a strong ATTRACTIVE beta ~ 2.3 (near the 2-D condensation
threshold of 2). => Real, physically meaningful lateral cohesion.
