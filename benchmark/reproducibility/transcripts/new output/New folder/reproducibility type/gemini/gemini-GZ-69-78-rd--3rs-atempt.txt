### 2. The van't Hoff (Gibbs-Helmholtz) Relationship

\frac{\partial}{\partial T} \left( \frac{\Delta G^\circ_{\text{mic}}}{T} \right) = -\frac{\Delta H^\circ_{\text{mic}}}{T^2}

\frac{d \ln(\text{CMC})}{dT} = \frac{\Delta H^\circ_{\text{mic}}}{R T^2}

Rearranging and integrating between two temperatures (T_1 and T_2) assuming \Delta H^\circ_{\text{mic}} is constant over this narrow temperature range:

Solving for \Delta H^\circ_{\text{mic}}:
