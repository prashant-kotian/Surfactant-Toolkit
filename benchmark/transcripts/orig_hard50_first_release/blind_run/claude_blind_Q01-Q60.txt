Q01 -- Ideal mixed CMC (Clint)

  alpha1=0.37, C1=1.23 mM, C2=8.76 mM.

Q02 -- Rubingh micellar mole fraction
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD: Solve Rubingh implicit eqn
  x1^2 ln(alpha1 C12 /(x1 C1)) = (1-x1)^2 ln((1-alpha1) C12 /((1-x1) C2)).
  alpha1=0.42, C12=2.31, C1=1.10, C2=9.80 mM -> x1 = 0.879876 (beta = +0.167).
ANSWER: x1 = 0.8799

Q03 -- Rubingh beta from a composition series
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD: For each (alpha1,C12) solve Rubingh for x1, then
  beta = ln(alpha1 C12/(x1 C1)) / (1-x1)^2; average the five values.
  C1=1.13, C2=6.71 mM. Per-point beta all ~ -3.6999 (series generated from
  constant beta) -> mean -3.6999699953.
ANSWER: mean beta = -3.69997
