Assumptions and Limitations
Tanford's empirical relations assume a fully extended (all-trans) saturated hydrocarbon chain; real chains have gauche defects, reducing effective length.
Geometric packing model (Israelachvili) treats the aggregate as a simple volume/area/length constraint, ignoring curvature elasticity, chain entropy, and specific interactions.
Electrostatic effects: SDS is ionic; the effective headgroup area depends strongly on ionic strength and counterion binding. The given A_min = 44.70 Å² reflects a particular set of solution conditions (likely no added salt, where electrostatic repulsion inflates the area). At higher ionic strength, A_min would shrink and P would increase.
Morphology discrepancy: SDS is experimentally known to form predominantly spherical micelles at dilute conditions.
