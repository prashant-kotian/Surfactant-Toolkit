Both scenarios use ΔS = (ΔH − ΔG) / T with ΔG = −37.131 kJ/mol and T = 298.15 K.

(a) ΔH = +0.904 kJ/mol
ΔS = (0.904 − (−37.131)) / 298.15 = 38.035 / 298.15 = 127.57 J/(mol·K)

(b) ΔH = −8.574 kJ/mol
ΔS = (−8.574 − (−37.131)) / 298.15 = 28.557 / 298.15 = 95.78 J/(mol·K)

Both are positive, consistent with micellization being entropy-driven in each case (the hydrophobic effect dominates), with the less exothermic/endothermic scenario (a) showing the larger entropy gain.
