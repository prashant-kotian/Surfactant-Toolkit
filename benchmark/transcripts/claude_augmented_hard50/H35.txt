Here are the results, computed with the surfactant-computation tools (no literature values used).

Debye length — from the ionic strength I = 0.0257 M at 298.15 K: λ_D = 1.8964 nm. (The quick estimate 0.304/√0.0257 = 1.896 nm agrees.)

κa — a/λ_D = 2.5 / 1.89637 = 1.3183 (dimensionless).

Zeta potential (Smoluchowski) — with the mobility 2.7×10⁻⁸ m²/(V·s) = 2.7 (µm·cm)/(V·s), η = 0.8903 mPa·s, and ε = ε₀·ε_r = 8.8542×10⁻¹² × 78.4: ζ = µ_e·η/ε = 34.63 mV.

One caveat worth flagging: κa ≈ 1.32 sits in the intermediate regime, where the Smoluchowski limit (valid for κa ≫ 1) overestimates ζ relative to the full Henry treatment — a Henry/Ohshima correction here would give a somewhat higher magnitude for the same mobility.
