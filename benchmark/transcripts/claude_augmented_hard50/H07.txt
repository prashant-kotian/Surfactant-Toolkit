ΔBIC = BIC(Langmuir) − BIC(Frumkin) = −40.360 − (−38.344) = −2.016. The sign is negative, meaning Langmuir has the lower BIC and is therefore preferred. The ~2-unit gap is the BIC penalty for Frumkin’s extra free parameter, which the fit doesn’t earn back.
Fitted Frumkin interaction parameter a = 0.0235. This is essentially zero — negligible lateral interaction between adsorbed molecules. A value this close to 0 means the Frumkin model has collapsed onto the Langmuir limit, so it does not represent a physically meaningful interaction.
Raw RSS: Frumkin’s R² (0.99988182) is a hair above Langmuir’s (0.99988088), so on unpenalized residuals Frumkin fits marginally better — which it must, having an extra parameter. That tiny improvement is what BIC correctly penalizes away.

In short: the data are cleanly Langmuir. Frumkin buys a vanishingly small RSS reduction with a near-zero interaction term, and BIC rejects it.
