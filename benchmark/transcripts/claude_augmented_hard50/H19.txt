Γ_max (Gibbs adsorption). Prefactor n = 1. The system has excess inert electrolyte, so the counterion activity is swamped (effectively constant) and only the surfactant ion’s chemical potential varies with concentration — this is the ionic_excess_electrolyte case. (n = 2 would apply only to a 1:1 ionic surfactant in pure water with no added salt.)

Γ_max = −(1/nRT)·dγ/dlnC = 7.42 × 10⁻⁶ mol/m²

A_min. A_min = 1/(N_A·Γ_max) = 0.224 nm²

ΔG_mic. Using the specified form ΔG = (1 + α)·RT·ln(CMC/55.5), with CMC = 0.008 mol/L and the 55.5 mol/L water conversion giving X_cmc = 1.44 × 10⁻⁴. Note that (1 + α) = (2 − β) with β = 1 − α = 0.70, so the prefactor 1.30 is the standard charged-micelle factor expressed via the dissociation degree.

ΔG_mic = 1.30 × RT × ln(1.44 × 10⁻⁴) = −28.5 kJ/mol
