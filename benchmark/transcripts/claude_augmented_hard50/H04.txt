Method applied at each row: ΔG_mic(T) = (2 − β)·R·T·ln(CMC/55.5) with β = 0.268, so the prefactor is 1.732. The eight ΔG points were fit to the curved form that allows a temperature-dependent enthalpy (ΔCp ≠ 0), referenced to 298.15 K.

Driver: At 298.15 K the enthalpy term is small and only slightly favorable (ΔH ≈ −2.85 kJ/mol), while the entropy term dominates the free energy (−TΔS ≈ −34.3 kJ/mol out of the total −37.2 kJ/mol). Micellization over this range is entropy-driven.
