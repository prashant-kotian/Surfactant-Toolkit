**Step 1: Theoretical Stationary State Estimation (Kinetic CMC and Nagg)**
In the isodesmic model where every sequential monomer addition $C_n + C_1 \rightleftharpoons C_{n+1}$ shares the same equilibrium constant $K$, the concentration of aggregates of size $n$ follows:
$$c_n = K^{n-1} c_1^n$$

The total concentration $c_{total}$ is the sum of monomers locked in all aggregates up to the maximum tracked size $n_{max} = 24$:
$$c_{total} = \sum_{n=1}^{24} n c_n = \sum_{n=1}^{24} n K^{n-1} c_1^n$$

Let $x = K c_1$. Substituting this in, we can solve for $x$:
$$c_{total} = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{n=1}^{24} n x^n \implies \sum_{n=1}^{24} n x^n = K \cdot c_{total}$$
$$\sum_{n=1}^{24} n x^n = 3300 \times 0.016 = 52.8$$

The geometric series for $\sum_{n=1}^{24} n x^n$ allows us to find $x$. 
For $N = 24$, the polynomial is $\sum_{n=1}^{24} n x^n = x \frac{1 - 25x^{24} + 24x^{25}}{(1-x)^2}$. 
Evaluating this root iteratively near the asymptote gives:
$x \approx 0.883976$

From this, the expected theoretical monomer concentration (CMC) is:
$$c_1 = \frac{x}{K} = \frac{0.883976}{3300} \approx 0.00026787 \text{ M} = 0.26787 \text{ mM}$$

Next, we determine the expected mean aggregation number over $n \ge 2$:
$$N_{agg} = \frac{\text{Total Monomers in Aggregates}}{\text{Total Number of Aggregates}} = \frac{\sum_{n=2}^{24} n x^n}{\sum_{n=2}^{24} x^n}$$
Numerator: $52.8 - x \approx 51.916$
Denominator: $\sum_{n=2}^{24} x^n = \frac{x^2 - x^{25}}{1-x} \approx \frac{0.7814 - 0.0457}{0.1160} \approx 6.342$
$$N_{agg} = \frac{51.916}{6.342} \approx 8.186$$
Rounding to the nearest integer yields:
$$N_{agg, rounded} = 8$$

**Step 2: Mass-Action Free Energy (dG)**
Using the derived expected averages:
*   $CMC_{kinetic, mM} \approx 0.26787$
*   $N_{agg} = 8$

$$X = \frac{0.26787 / 1000}{(0.26787 / 1000) + 55.5} \approx \frac{0.00026787}{55.50026787} \approx 4.82647 \times 10^{-6}$$

$$dG = \frac{R \cdot T}{1000} \times \left( \left(1 + \frac{1}{N_{agg}}\right) \ln(X) - \frac{1}{N_{agg}} \ln(N_{agg}) \right)$$
$$dG = \frac{8.314462618 \times 298.15}{1000} \times \left( 1.125 \times \ln(4.82647 \times 10^{-6}) - 0.125 \times \ln(8) \right)$$
$$dG = 2.47895 \times \left( 1.125 \times (-12.24125) - 0.125 \times 2.07944 \right)$$
$$dG = 2.47895 \times \left( -13.77141 - 0.25993 \right) = 2.47895 \times (-14.03134) \approx -34.783 \text{ kJ/mol}$$
