### Question H31

#### Principles and Calculations
1. **Prefactor in the Gibbs Adsorption Isotherm**:
   The Gibbs adsorption isotherm is expressed as:
   $$d\gamma = -\sum_{i} \Gamma_i d\mu_i$$
   For a $1:1$ ionic surfactant ($R^+ X^-$ or $R^- M^+$) in pure aqueous solution with **no added electrolyte**, electroneutrality requires equal adsorption of surfactant ions and counterions at the interface ($\Gamma_+ = \Gamma_- = \Gamma$). In the bulk, their concentrations change simultaneously ($C_+ = C_- = C$), so:
   $$d\mu = d\mu_+ + d\mu_- = R T d\ln(a_+) + R T d\ln(a_-) \approx 2 R T d\ln C$$
   Hence, the Gibbs prefactor is $n = 2$.
   Relating surface tension to $\log_{10} C$:
   $$\frac{d\gamma}{d\ln C} = \frac{1}{\ln(10)} \frac{d\gamma}{d\log_{10} C}$$
   $$\Gamma_{\max} = -\frac{1}{n R T \ln(10)} \frac{d\gamma}{d\log_{10} C}$$

2. **Maximum Surface Excess ($\Gamma_{\max}$)**:
   Given $\frac{d\gamma}{d\log_{10} C} = -9.2\text{ mN/m} = -9.2 \times 10^{-3}\text{ N/m}$, $T = 298.15\text{ K}$, and $R = 8.314462618\text{ J/(mol}\cdot\text{K)}$:
   $$n R T \ln(10) = 2 \times 8.314462618 \times 298.15 \times \ln(10) \approx 11416.024\text{ J/mol}$$
   $$\Gamma_{\max} = \frac{9.2 \times 10^{-3}\text{ N/m}}{11416.024\text{ J/mol}} = 8.05885 \times 10^{-7}\text{ mol/m}^2$$

3. **Minimum Area per Molecule ($A_{\min}$)**:
   Using Avogadro's constant $N_A = 6.02214076 \times 10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1}$:
   $$A_{\min} = \frac{1}{N_A \Gamma_{\max}} = \frac{10^{18}}{6.02214076 \times 10^{23} \times 8.05885 \times 10^{-7}} = 2.0605\text{ nm}^2$$

4. **Standard Free Energy of Micellization ($\Delta G_{\text{mic}}$)**:
   With $\text{CMC} = 6.0\text{ mM} = 6.0 \times 10^{-3}\text{ mol/L}$ and $\alpha = 0.30$:
   $$X = \frac{\text{CMC}}{55.5} = \frac{6.0 \times 10^{-3}}{55.5} = 1.081081 \times 10^{-4}$$
   $$\ln(X) = -9.13240$$
   $$\Delta G_{\text{mic}} = (1 + \alpha) R T \ln(X) = 1.30 \times 8.314462618 \times 298.15 \times (-9.13240) \times 10^{-3} = -29.43\text{ kJ/mol}$$

### Question H32

#### Principles and Calculations
1. **Naive Stokes-Einstein Hydrodynamic Radius ($R_{h,\text{naive}}$)**:
   From the Stokes-Einstein relation for translational diffusion of a spherical particle:
   $$D = \frac{k_B T}{6\pi \eta R_h} \implies R_{h,\text{naive}} = \frac{k_B T}{6\pi \eta D}$$
   Given:
   - $T = 298.15\text{ K}$
   - Boltzmann constant $k_B = 1.380649 \times 10^{-23}\text{ J/K}$
   - Solvent viscosity $\eta = 0.8903 \times 10^{-3}\text{ Pa}\cdot\text{s}$
   - Diffusion coefficient $D = 7.5 \times 10^{-11}\text{ m}^2/\text{s}$

   $$k_B T = 1.380649 \times 10^{-23} \times 298.15 = 4.116405 \times 10^{-21}\text{ J}$$
   $$6\pi \eta D = 6\pi \times (0.8903 \times 10^{-3}) \times (7.5 \times 10^{-11}) = 1.258623 \times 10^{-12}\text{ N}\cdot\text{s/m}$$
   $$R_{h,\text{naive}} = \frac{4.116405 \times 10^{-21}}{1.258623 \times 10^{-12}} = 3.27056 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m} = 3.2706\text{ nm}$$

2. **Perrin Friction Factor and Correction**:
   For a prolate ellipsoid of revolution with axial ratio $p = a/b = 1.14 > 1$, the Perrin frictional ratio $F_p = f / f_0$ relative to an equivalent sphere of the same volume is:
   $$F_p = \frac{\sqrt{p^2 - 1}}{p^{1/3} \ln\left(p + \sqrt{p^2 - 1}\right)}$$
   Evaluating at $p = 1.14$:
   $$\sqrt{p^2 - 1} = \sqrt{1.14^2 - 1} = \sqrt{0.2996} = 0.547357$$
   $$p + \sqrt{p^2 - 1} = 1.14 + 0.547357 = 1.687357 \implies \ln(1.687357) = 0.523165$$
   $$p^{1/3} = (1.14)^{1/3} = 1.044648$$
   $$F_p = \frac{0.547357}{1.044648 \times 0.523165} = \frac{0.547357}{0.546522} = 1.001533$$

   The Perrin friction-corrected hydrodynamic radius (equivalent sphere radius) is:
   $$R_{h,\text{Perrin}} = \frac{R_{h,\text{naive}}}{F_p} = \frac{3.27056}{1.001533} = 3.2655\text{ nm}$$

### Question H33

#### Principles and Calculations
1. **Unit Conversions and Velocity**:
   - Displacing fluid viscosity: $\mu = 12.0\text{ cP} = 12.0 \times 10^{-3}\text{ Pa}\cdot\text{s} = 0.012\text{ Pa}\cdot\text{s}$
   - Interfacial tension: $\gamma = 32.0\text{ mN/m} = 32.0 \times 10^{-3}\text{ N/m} = 0.032\text{ N/m}$
   - Darcy velocity: $v = 0.30\text{ ft/day}$
     Using $1\text{ ft} = 0.3048\text{ m}$ and $1\text{ day} = 86400\text{ s}$:
     $$v = \frac{0.30 \times 0.3048}{86400}\text{ m/s} = 1.058333 \times 10^{-6}\text{ m/s}$$

2. **Capillary Number ($\text{Ca}$)**:
   $$\text{Ca} = \frac{\mu v}{\gamma} = \frac{0.012\text{ Pa}\cdot\text{s} \times 1.058333 \times 10^{-6}\text{ m/s}}{0.032\text{ N/m}} = 3.96875 \times 10^{-7}$$

3. **Threshold Comparison**:
   The onset threshold for viscous fingering is $\text{Ca}^* = 1 \times 10^{-4}$.
   Since $3.96875 \times 10^{-7} < 1 \times 10^{-4}$, $\text{Ca}$ does **not** exceed the threshold (`false`).

4. **Velocity Required for $\text{Ca} = 10^{-4}$**:
   $$v^* = \frac{\text{Ca}^* \gamma}{\mu} = \frac{10^{-4} \times 0.032}{0.012} = 2.666667 \times 10^{-4}\text{ m/s}$$
   Converting back to $\text{ft/day}$:
   $$v^* = 2.666667 \times 10^{-4}\text{ m/s} \times \frac{86400\text{ s/day}}{0.3048\text{ m/ft}} = 75.5906\text{ ft/day}$$

### Question H34

#### Principles and Calculations
1. **Bulk Mole Fraction from Weight Composition**:
   Components: SDS ($1$, $M_1 = 288.38\text{ g/mol}$, $C_1^* = 8.3\text{ mM}$) and DTAB ($2$, $M_2 = 308.34\text{ g/mol}$, $C_2^* = 15.6\text{ mM}$).

   $$n_2 = \frac{35.0\text{ g}}{308.34\text{ g/mol}} = 0.1135111\text{ mol}$$
   $$x_1 = \alpha_1 = \frac{n_1}{n_1 + n_2} = \frac{0.2254005}{0.3389116} = 0.665068$$

2. **Ideal Mixed CMC (Clint Equation)**:
   $$\frac{1}{C_{\text{lint}}} = \frac{\alpha_1}{C_1^*} + \frac{1 - \alpha_1}{C_2^*} = \frac{0.665068}{8.3} + \frac{0.334932}{15.6} = 0.0801287 + 0.0214700 = 0.1015987\text{ mM}^{-1}$$
   $$C_{\text{lint}} = \frac{1}{0.1015987} = 9.8426\text{ mM}$$

3. **Percent Deviation of Measured CMC from Ideal**:
   With $C_{\text{meas}} = 8.10\text{ mM}$:
   $$\text{Deviation (\%)} = \frac{C_{\text{lint}} - C_{\text{meas}}}{C_{\text{meas}}} \times 100 = \frac{9.8426 - 8.10}{8.10} \times 100 = +21.5142\%$$

4. **Rubingh Micellar Mole Fraction ($x_1$) and Interaction Parameter ($\beta$)**:
   Rubingh's regular-solution coexistence equation:
   $$x_1^2 \ln\left(\frac{\alpha_1 C_{\text{mix}}}{x_1 C_1^*}\right) = (1 - x_1)^2 \ln\left(\frac{(1 - \alpha_1) C_{\text{mix}}}{(1 - x_1) C_2^*}\right)$$

   $$\frac{\alpha_1 C_{\text{mix}}}{C_1^*} = \frac{0.665068 \times 8.10}{8.3} = 0.649042$$
   $$\frac{(1 - \alpha_1) C_{\text{mix}}}{C_2^*} = \frac{0.334932 \times 8.10}{15.6} = 0.173907$$
   Solving for $x_1 \in (0, 1)$ numerically gives:
   $$x_{1,\text{rubingh}} = 0.708424$$
   The regular solution interaction parameter $\beta$ is then:
   $$\beta = \frac{\ln\left(\frac{0.649042}{0.708424}\right)}{(1 - 0.708424)^2} = \frac{-0.087532}{0.085016} = -1.0297$$

   $$\Delta H_{\text{mic}} = R \times 1000 \times \left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)\text{ J/mol} = R \times \left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)\text{ kJ/mol}$$
   Notice that the factor of $1000$ from the abscissa units ($1000/T$) exactly cancels the factor of $1000$ when converting $\text{J/mol}$ to $\text{kJ/mol}$:
   $$\Delta H_{\text{mic}} = 8.314462618 \times (-0.30092) = -2.502\text{ kJ/mol}$$
