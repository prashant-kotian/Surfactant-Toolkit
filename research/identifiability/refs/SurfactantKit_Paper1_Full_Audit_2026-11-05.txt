1. Publication decision and main recommendation 
The present manuscript is not ready for submission. Its main difficulty is scientific validity 
and identification of the measured parameter, rather than language or formatting. The current 
positive inversion claim is unsupported by an external experiment. The later notebook's opposite 
conclusion, that real micellar systems occupy an impossible inversion regime, is also unsupported. 
A mismatched, poor, boundary -limited fit to six digitized points cannot establish a universal 
physical impossibility. 
The strongest route is a substantial scientific reset: establish which kinetic time the 
equations actually contain; repair the equilibrium concentration and reservoir conventions; derive 
the linear transfer function; then determine when full, measured complex spectra identify that 
time against competing physical and instrumental explanations. Retain large amplitude only if it 
adds demonstrated information under equal measurement time and calibrated noise. A publishable 
paper could emerge as a validated iden tification method or as a carefully bounded identifiability 
study. Neither outcome should be chosen before the repaired analysis. 
Several useful components survive: a runnable reaction –diffusion solver, locally 
conservative exchange, a numerical test suite, documented reversals of earlier claims, and a 
potentially useful sensitivity map. These are research assets, not evidence that the manuscript's 
numerical recommendations are correct. The public library is present in the supplied archive; a 
prior assertion that it was private or unavailable must not be repeated.

1.2 Severity and confidence 
P0 means the issue blocks the central scientific interpretation. P1 means a major 
validation, inference, or novelty problem. P2 means a reporting or submission defect. A verified 
finding follows directly from the supplied artifact or an independent check. A model -dependent 
deduction follows from the stated equations and is not an experimental observation. A required 
investigation identifies an unresolved physical assumption. This distinction matters because 
several earlier errors arose when a conditional mo del result was promoted to a fact about real 
surfactants. 
2. Foundational model problems 
2.1 P0 The parameter called tau two is not automatically the bulk relaxation time 
Verified mathematical deduction. In model.py the exchange equations, with diffusion 
suppressed, are dc/dt = (M − C_m c)/tau and dM/dt = (C_m c − M)/tau. The linear Jacobian has 
eigenvalues zero and −(1 + C_m)/tau. Therefore the conserved, closed two -pool disturbance 
relaxes with time tau/(1 + C_m), rather than tau. At the advertised C_m = 47.5 and tau = 4 s, this 

Page 4 
is 0.08247 s. Calling the coefficient 4 s the Aniansson –Wall slow relaxation time requires a 
derivation and is presently unjustified. 
Fix: temporarily name the coefficient tau_exchange. Derive its mapping to experimentally 
measured kinetic eigenmodes from a consistent mass-action or reduced Aniansson–Wall model. If 
the intended closed relaxation time is tau_slow, a two -pool version needs an exchange prefactor 
consistent with that definition, for example (1 + C_m) tau_slow in the denominator. This 
illustrative correction does not itself establish a valid Aniansson –Wall reduction. Recompute 
instrument horizons only after the mapping is settled. Source: model.py, run_oscillation; Danov et 
al. part 1, Section 4 [2]. 
2.2 P0 Equilibrium reservoir inventory is confused with its susceptibility 
Verified implementation issue. The code initializes M0 = C_m c0. But C_m = dM_eq/dc 
is a derivative, not the ratio M_eq/c. In the monodisperse mass -action closure M_eq is 
proportional to c raised to the aggregation number n, and C_m = n M_eq/c. Thus M_eq = C_m 
c/n. The offset written in the model currently vanishes identically because M0 is set to C_m c0. 
At the nominal 1.5 CMC point the code stores c0 = 0.1065 mol per cubic metre and M0 = 
5.05875, giving c0 + M0 = 5.16525 against nominal total 0.1065. With pinned monomer 0.071, 
the physical micellized inventory would instead be 0.0355. The inflated inventory can hide 
depletion or positivity problems and dilutes relative mass -error normalization. It does not, by 
itself, change the linear perturbation dynamics because constant offsets cancel. Fix the base 
inventory and susceptibility separately, retain the co rrect nonzero linearization offset, and test 
physical pool positivity. 
2.3 P0 Total concentration is used as monomer concentration above the CMC 
Verified. SurfactantSystem.to_model passes c_total_mM into ModelParams.c0; key study 
scripts do the same. The transport variable c is explicitly monomer concentration. Above CMC, 
increasing nominal total concentration therefore also increases the Langmuir monomer 

Page 5 
concentration and coverage, while independently adding a micellar reservoir. This mixes two 
equilibrium conventions and confounds the reported concentration optimum. 
An illustrative independent linear calculation at fixed K = 140, C_m = 47.5, tau = 4 and 
angular frequency 6.28 gives phase 67.98 degrees using c0 = 0.1065, versus 60.74 degrees with 
c0 pinned to 0.071. This is an internal sensitivity comparison, not a corrected experimental 
prediction. Fix by solving total = c_eq + M_eq(c_eq) or using a justified pinned -monomer 
approximation; evaluate adsorption at c_eq, and vary reservoir inventory and slope consistently. 
2.4 P0 The claimed need for large amplitude is contradicted by the model 
Verified independent reproduction. At the advertised parameter set, strain 0.002 gives 
phase 67.9764 degrees at angular frequency 6.28; strain 0.20 gives 67.9460 degrees. At angular 
frequency 0.31416 the corresponding values are 69.5760 and 69.6233 degrees. An analytic linear 
response predicts 67.9766 and 69.5670 degrees. Thus the central large phase exists in the 
infinitesimal-strain model. It is not created by higher harmonics or isotherm nonlinearity. 
The manuscript itself partly acknowledges amplitude independence in Section 4.5 but 
retains a large-amplitude title, abstract, and detection requirement. Fix: make the linear theory the 
baseline. Demonstrate an improvement from nonlinear forcing in information, independent 
parameter separation, or held -out prediction before describing LAOD as necessary. Higher signal 
at larger strain can be useful experimentally, but does not establish a new nonlinear identification 
mechanism. 
2.5 P1 The analytic linear solution should replace much of the numerical exploration 
Independent derivation for the supplied immobile -reservoir model. With time dependence 
exp(i omega t), define h = dGamma_eq/dc and B(omega) = 1 + C_m/(1 + i omega tau_exchange). 
The decaying bulk mode has q squared = i omega B/D. The modulus is E = E0 i omega/[i omega 
+ D q/h]. Select the square -root branch with positive real part. The phase convention must be 
stated explicitly. These equations reproduce the numerical small -strain phase to approximately 
0.01 degrees at the two checked frequencies. 

Page 6 
This provides a fast independent reference, exposes the governing groups, and permits 
broad profile likelihoods without repeated expensive PDE fits. It also explains why a micellar 
phase above 45 degrees need not imply a nonlinear interface. Verify amplitude as well as phase, 
derive limiting cases, and compare the finite -amplitude solver against this expression over a 
preregistered grid. Novelty must concern the identification result or validated physical extension, 
not merely obtaining this transfer function. 
2.6 P1 Micellar diffusion cannot be dismissed because micelles are slower 
Required physical investigation with an analytic warning. The manuscript claims micellar 
diffusion matters only at the highest frequencies. Fainerman's primary treatment explicitly 
includes micelle diffusion with D_m/D about 0.25 [1]. A smaller diffusion coefficient does not 
establish a negligible contribution when the transported micellar inventory and susceptibility are 
large. 
In the local -equilibrium limit, a two -diffusing-pool model has total storage 1 + C_m and 
effective transport coefficient D + C_m D_m. Its admittance is proportional to the square root of 
(D + C_m D_m)(1 + C_m), whereas the immobile approximation uses the square root of D(1 + 
C_m). Their ratio is sqrt(1 + C_m D_m/D): approximately 3.59 at C_m = 47.5 and 8.89 at C_m = 
312 for ratio 0.25. Boundary conditions and species definitions still require care. Implement the 
two-species transport model and map its valid immobile limit before interpreting fitted capacities. 
2.7 P1 Local exchange cancellation is not a verified global mass balance 
Verified diagnostic limitation. The exchange terms cancel locally, which is a real strength. 
However mass_error is the endpoint change of the unweighted integral of c + M plus Gamma, 
divided by initial inventory. It does not integrate the far -boundary flux. It also does not account 
explicitly for changing surface area or the associated bulk geometry. For the implemented fixed -
grid equations, differentiating that sum gives the far -boundary flux minus Gamma dlnA/dt; it is 
not generally zero. 

Page 7 
Fix: derive the control -volume balance for the chosen geometry, including area -weighted 
surface amount and any bulk deformation or advection. Integrate all physical fluxes and calculate 
the residual of that identity. Report both absolute residual and a normalization relevant to 
exchanged surfactant. Separately test mesh, domain extent, and periodic convergence. The 
reported roughly 0.2 percent endpoint drift is neither a proof of conservation nor automatically a 
numerical error. 
2.8 P1 A single reservoir variable has not been established as the slow micellar mode 
Required investigation. Danov et al. explicitly distinguish micelle number, mean 
aggregation number, and polydispersity, with multiple characteristic times [2]. Fainerman 
distinguishes fast monomer release from slow complete dissolution [1]. A single pool variable can 
be a useful reduced model, but its equivalence to the slow mode is an additional hypothesis. The 
orientation's question about equating beta with C_m is resolved only after specifying the kinetic 
process: beta is an excess -concentration ratio, while the slow equilibrium susceptibility can 
include n beta. They are not universally interchangeable. 
Fix: derive a controlled reduction with stated timescale separation, or label the fitted 
parameter an effective reservoir time. Use the fuller model to test whether the one -pool null locus 
survives. A good fit alone cannot assign an effective time to a microscopic process. 
2.9 P1 A change of numerical state variable cannot make the physics infinitely compressible 
Verified conceptual error in manuscript Section 2.3. For an invertible local isotherm 
Gamma = f(c_s), the surface balance can be written f prime(c_s) dc_s/dt = j − f(c_s) dlnA/dt. This 
is equivalent to integrating Gamma and inverting f. Carrying c_s as a differential state is not 
inherently wrong; omitting the interfacial storage derivative or mixing constraints is wrong. 
Fix the explanation and add an equivalence check between the two valid formulations. 
Remove the unsupported suggestion that standard choices of state variable create infinite 
compressibility. Retaining this argument would undermine a reviewer’s confidence in the 
derivation.

2.10 P2 Additional parameter and utility defects 
Verified from systems.py and model.py. The volume -fraction expression multiplies 
concentration in mM, numerically equal to mol per cubic metre, by an extra factor 0.001 before 
using density in kilograms per cubic metre. Under the stated units this makes the estimated 
volume fraction 1000 times too small; a true partial -volume model is preferable. The default 
C12E8 tau2_ms range is 1 –10 milliseconds, while the paper uses 4 seconds. The to_model 
argument c_m_tau is multiplied by 0.001 despite a name and defau lt that do not clearly declare 
milliseconds. 
Also, dgamma_dcs returns an adsorption derivative in its Langmuir branch but a surface -
tension derivative in its Frumkin branch, so its units differ across branches. This was not shown to 
alter the audited Langmuir runs, but it is unsafe for extensions. Fix names, units, documentation, 
and input guards; test inconsistent cycle counts, nonpositive frequency or relaxation time, invalid 
strain, and depleted reservoirs before broadening the paper.

3.2 P0 The external comparison does not match the experiment 
Verified. Fainerman studied C14EO8, at 25 degrees Celsius, with 5 percent area 
oscillations, a buoyant bubble, and a combined reorientation/compression adsorption model [1]. 
The fit script uses C12E8 parameters, strain 0.20, a Langmuir interface, no micellar diffusion, and 
monomer concentration C_TOT = (30/7) times the C12E8 CMC. That is approximately 0.3043 
mol per cubic metre, rather than the experiment's C14EO8 monomer CMC near 0.007. Its nominal 
monomer concentration is about 43 times that value. 
Fix: use the actual species, concentration convention, experimental amplitude, geometry, 
and independently calibrated adsorption slope. Digitize modulus and phase jointly. A homolog 
transfer may be an exploratory stress test, but cannot support a quantitative correction of the real 
reservoir capacity or a universal inversion limit. 
3.3 P1 A poor fit does not measure an effective capacity reliably 
Verified statistical correction. With SSE 193.1815, the conventional root mean square 
residual over six observations is 5.6742 degrees. The archived 6.9495 is sqrt(SSE/4), a residual 
standard deviation with two fitted parameters, not the same quantity. If the stated one -degree 
uncertainty were an independent Gaussian standard deviation, chi -square is about 193 for four 
residual degrees of freedom: a severe lack of fit. The notebook's statement that approximately six 
degrees is barely above one-degree noise is incorrect. 
Fix: distinguish digitization uncertainty from original experimental uncertainty, expand 
parameter ranges, profile nuisance parameters, inspect residuals, and establish model adequacy 
before interpreting parameters. A boundary optimum can reflect inadequate search range, 
mismatch, or non-identifiability; it does not uniquely diagnose any one of them. 
3.4 P1 Digitization is plausible visually but not calibrated or independently repeated 
Primary Figure 5 on printed page 6451 was visually inspected. The supplied six points 
roughly follow the filled -square 30 micromolar series, so there is no basis here to call the entire 
curve fabricated. However the script says they were read by eye, and no second extraction,

coordinate calibration, pixel uncertainty, or provenance image accompanies them. Dense 
overlapping symbols make uncertainty important. 
Fix: retain a calibrated extraction project, symbol coordinates, figure crop, and an 
independent second extraction. Obtain author data if possible. Distinguish reading error from 
scatter in the original measurements; do not assume that repeated digitization creates independent

3.5 P0 One local sensitivity zero is not a global impossibility theorem 
Model-dependent deduction. The notebook promotes a zero of the derivative of one phase 
observable into statements about impossibility for real systems. A local zero at one frequency 
need not imply zero information in amplitude, other frequencies, transients, or nonlinear 
components. A flat first derivative can retain higher -order dependence and can also create 
multiple branches rather than total non-identifiability. 
Fix: construct the full observation Jacobian after nuisance parameters are included, 
examine its singular values, and compute profile likelihoods and disconnected minima over the 
permitted parameter domain. State conclusions for a specific observation set, error model and 
frequency window. The current evidence supports a conditional sensitivity limitation inside one 
model, not that real surfactants cannot be inverted. 
3.6 P1 Do not force tau two to be constant across concentration 
Required model investigation. Protocol B jointly fits multiple concentrations while 
assuming one common kinetic time. Classical micellar relaxation times can depend on 
concentration and micellar distribution [2]. A common time can therefore manufacture apparent 
information or bias even if capacity is correct. Fix by comparing a mechanistic concentration law, 
a local constant approximation over a justified narrow range, and concentration -specific times. 
Use held-out concentration prediction; additional concentrations are useful only when their shared 
assumptions are defensible.

4. Measurement and inference need redesign 
4.1 P1 The code reports phase advance while calling it lag 
Verified unit check. first_harmonic_phase uses y = amplitude cos(theta − phi). A response 
delayed by 30 degrees has phi_gamma − phi_area = positive 30 degrees. lag_deg subtracts in the 
reverse order and returns 330 degrees for that test. The numerical model's positive reported phase 
matches the positive argument of the analytic complex modulus, namely a stress phase advance in 
that convention. This can be a valid loss-angle convention, but it is not the stated temporal lag. 
Fix the naming and convention throughout, test a known lead and delay, state the Fourier 
sign, and compare complex moduli using the primary paper's convention. Avoid converting every 
phase to the interval zero to 360 for residual calculations: use complex data or circular differences 
to prevent artificial discontinuities. 
4.2 P1 The five-frame rule is an estimator-specific aliasing result 
Verified rank check. harmonics.py defaults to four harmonics, which requires nine Fourier 
coefficients including the mean. Exactly phase -locked samples at five distinct phases per cycle 
have matrix rank five, even over repeated cycles. Nine or more distinct phases are needed for that 
unrestricted nine -coefficient fit. The separate fundamental -phase fit requires fewer coefficients, 
but omitted higher harmonics can alias into it. 
Fix: derive sampling requirements from the fitted harmonic order, exposure time, 
synchronization, timestamps and noise. One sample per cycle is generally unidentifiable for a 
phase-locked fundamental; arbitrary returned angles are not meaningful biased estimates. Five 
frames can work for a restricted waveform, but is not a universal LAOD specification. Validate 
the actual acquisition chain rather than inferring it from nominal camera frame rate. 
4.3 P1 Synthetic noise is sampled at a numerical rate rather than a verified instrument rate 
Verified. study_recovery_uncertainty.py uses run_oscillation's default 320 samples per 
cycle, retaining four cycles. The advertised PAT rate is 25 –50 frames per second, and the four 
angular frequencies correspond to approximately 0.48–1 Hz. The synthetic 320 samples per cycle 

Page 12 
represent approximately 153 –320 samples per second. White noise attached independently to 
every solver output therefore supplies more independent measurements than the proposed 
instrument. 
At 1 Hz, four retained cycles give 1280 synthetic readings against 100 –200 at 25–50 fps. 
Under an ideal independent -noise approximation alone, uncertainty could grow by factors 
sqrt(1280/200) to sqrt(1280/100), approximately 2.53–3.58. These are illustrative scaling factors, 
not corrected precision estimates. Resample at actual timestamps, include exposure averaging and 
measured temporal correlation, and repeat recovery under the same total experiment time. 
4.4 P1 Covariance calculation and uncertainty reporting contain specific errors 
Verified in study_recovery_uncertainty.py. The covariance fit is unweighted across 
frequencies despite different Monte Carlo phase standard deviations, and uses a scalar mean noise 
estimate. The capacity parameter is fitted in logarithmic coordinates, so sqrt(covariance diagonal) 
is already an approximate relative uncertainty; dividing it again by capacity in capacity_relSE is 
incorrect. Seeds are reused across frequency noise calculations, which requires an explicit cross -
frequency covariance treatment before treating samples as independent. 
Fix with a declared likelihood in raw waveform or complex -coefficient space, covariance 
estimated from calibration, proper transformations, and either independent random streams or 
intentional, documented correlations. Do not claim that a single frequency can never yield 
uncertainty because residual degrees of freedom vanish: a known noise model, replicated 
observations, or a raw waveform can supply uncertainty without residual variance estimation from 
one scalar. 
4.5 P1 Conditional synthetic precision is not measurement accuracy 
Verified limitation. Data generated by the same forward model and fitted with that model 
test algorithmic recovery under assumed conditions. They do not validate the physical model. 
Sixty draws reporting 90 percent coverage have Monte Carlo standard error about 3.9 percentage 

Page 13 
points, so the precise size of the coverage defect is uncertain, even though the optimistic 
covariance deserves attention. 
Fix: simulate from competing models, alternate solvers and perturbed calibrations, and 
repeat enough trials to estimate coverage adequately. For example, about 1900 independent trials 
give approximately 0.5 percentage -point standard error near 95 percent coverage, subject to the 
study design. Report bias, interval width, coverage, rejected fits and failures together. Increasing 
retained cycles is a legitimate design change, but requires a fresh uncertainty study and explicit 
measurement-time cost. 
4.6 P1 Second-harmonic detectability is not necessary for phase identification 
Verified logical issue. The manuscript first shows that the fundamental phase contains the 
useful information even at small amplitude, then requires I2 to exceed a threshold before allowing 
inversion. This excludes informative fundamental data by an unrelated nonlinear -signal condition 
and creates part of the apparent concentration window. 
Fix: define usable measurements through information or likelihood in the chosen 
observables. If the method uses only the fundamental, require sufficient fundamental signal, 
calibrated phase uncertainty and adequate sensitivity. If nonlinear harmonics are proposed as 
added information, quantify their incremental contribution and propagated covariance. Recompute 
the window after removing the unsupported I2 prerequisite. 
4.7 P1 The ratio and phase are compared with inconsistent noise accounting 
Verified methodological concern. The harmonic -ratio argument propagates an individual 
instrument reading error into a ratio, whereas the strongest phase precision averages many 
synthetic readings. Both estimators should be compared after the same acquisition, averaging and 
calibration. A ratio's uncertainty also depends on numerator and denominator covariance and 
signal magnitude. 
Fix: jointly fit the complex fundamental and higher harmonics from each waveform, 
estimate coefficient covariance, and compare phase -only, amplitude-only, ratio-only and full-data 

Page 14 
inference under equal budgets. Do not conclude that any published harmonic -ratio inversion must 
be underdetermined merely because this particular benchmark is weak. 
4.8 P1 Nuisance mechanisms must compete with reservoir kinetics 
Required validation. Include adsorption barriers, surface reorientation/compression, finite -
radius transport, drift, baseline history, actual area forcing, exposure filtering, and instrument 
timing. A time delay t_delay adds a phase contribution of magnitude omega t_delay and can 
resemble a kinetic trend. Area and tension inferred from the same drop images can have 
correlated errors. 
Fix: calibrate timing and area response independently, fit complex spectra and waveform 
residuals, and test nuisance models with held -out protocols. Al-Sou'od and Ghanem already show 
why relaxation assignments can disappear when drift or forcing uncertainty is admitted [4]. Their 
result does not prove this model wrong, but supplies a necessary comparison standard. 
5. Novelty and literature claims need correction 
5.1 P1 The 45-degree ceiling and its measured violation are established prior work 
Verified primary evidence. Fainerman et al. explicitly state the planar diffusion -model 
maximum of 45 degrees and discuss measured excess in 2008 [1], printed page 6451. 
Recomputing that limit is a consistency check, not a new theorem, and exceeding it cannot 
uniquely identify a slow lagging micellar reservoir. The same paper lists finite fast kinetics, finite

attribution to a sign difference between fast and lagging mechanisms is too strong. 
Fix: say that an excess rejects a specified planar instantaneous -equilibrium diffusion 
baseline under its validity conditions. Use independent mechanisms and geometry to test 
microscopic attribution. Do not advertise a parameter-free proof of micelles or of slow kinetics.

7. Recommended new scientific approach 
7.1 Frame the question before choosing a positive or negative answer 
Ask: Under which experimental protocols can a micellar kinetic time be separated from 
equilibrium capacity, monomer and micelle transport, adsorption physics and instrumental 
uncertainty? Define the intended microscopic time and the admissible parameter domain. This 
question is stronger than whether a simulated phase can be fitted, and more defensible than 
announcing universal non-recoverability from one external curve. 
Two paths can support a strong paper. A positive methods paper requires external 
recovery and prediction with independent kinetic comparison. A bounding paper requires a 
reproducible limitation for stated models, observables and noise, plus a demonstrated way to 
escape it or a carefully quantified inability to do so. A single failed fit or a familiar 45 -degree 
asymptote is insufficient for either path. 
7.2 Build a model hierarchy with observable distinctions 
Use a planar diffusion baseline; a mobile -micelle equilibrium model that recovers the 
appropriate effective-D limit; a finite micelle -kinetics model with a derived parameter mapping; 

Page 19 
and a small set of physically relevant alternatives such as adsorption barriers or surface 
reorientation. Add spherical transport and instrument filtering where dimensionless checks require 
them. Derive nested limits and thermodynamic stability rather than choosing a more elaborate 
model because it fits better. 
For each model, predict both storage and loss components, phase and modulus, plus time 
traces when available. Use measured equilibrium adsorption data to constrain E0 and h. Keep 
identical nuisance treatment across models. Test whether the extra kinetic parameter produces 
identifiable, predictive behavior; do not infer microscopic reality from a lower training residual 
alone. 
7.3 Design experiments to separate parameters 
First measure equilibrium surface tension against concentration, CMC, purity, 
temperature, and instrument timing response. Establish an amplitude range where the fundamental 
response is reproducible and the drop or bubble remains stable. Start with small -amplitude 
frequency sweeps spanning below and above the candidate kinetic feature. Choose frequencies 
from the full sensitivity matrix, not only from the largest phase derivative at one nominal truth. 
Measure several concentrations only with a defensible concentration dependence of the 
kinetic model. Repeat selected frequencies and amplitudes, randomize sweep order, and record 
actual area, tension, drop volume, timestamps and equilibrium history. Reserve complete 
frequencies, concentrations or forcing protocols before fitting. Use a step or broadband protocol if 
it distinguishes otherwise degenerate parameters more efficiently. A second instrument is justified 
only if its calibrated useful band resolves an established feature. 
7.4 Make large amplitude earn its place 
Compare small strain, intermediate strain and approximately 0.20 under equal total 
acquisition time, actual frame rate and calibrated noise. Evaluate extra information from higher 
harmonics and waveform shape after allowing the same nuisance models. Compare bias under 
deliberate model mismatch, not just smaller nominal covariance. Use matched fundamental 

Page 20 
amplitude or measurement -time comparisons to distinguish a nonlinear advantage from ordinary 
signal averaging. 
If the large-amplitude waveform does not resolve a degeneracy that small -strain complex 
spectra cannot, remove LAOD from the central novelty claim. If it does, demonstrate that gain on 
held-out physical data and identify the mechanism of the gain. This is the decisive test for 
retaining the original paper concept. 
7.5 Use inference that can reveal failure 
Fit log-positive parameters with a declared noise likelihood, inspect global and multistart 
solutions, profile the physical kinetic time after nuisance parameters are optimized, and report 
disconnected intervals where they occur. Use singular values as a local diagnostic and profile 
likelihood or posterior exploration as the global check. Treat bounded fits explicitly as bounds, 
rather than central estimates with symmetric errors. 
Compare predictive error on locked data; use information criteria only with a defensible 
likelihood and effective observation count. Raw frames, correlated Fourier coefficients and 
digitized curve points are not interchangeable independent observations. Report calibration 
uncertainty, systematic model sensitivity, bias, repeatability and between -run reproducibility 
separately from conditional fitting precision. 
8. Concrete revision plan and acceptance gates 
8.1 Stage one Repair and freeze the foundations 
Resolve the kinetic eigenmode definition; separate c_eq, total concentration, M_eq and 
dM_eq/dc; derive the transfer function and global balance; correct phase terminology and 
parameter units. Reproduce the analytic and numerical limits across concentration, frequency and 
capacity. Generate a new claims ledger and retire numbers from the old operating -window study. 
Acceptance gate: equilibrium mass closes, the intended kinetic time matches the reduced model's 
eigenmode, and analytic and numerical results agree within the declared numerical error. 

Page 21 
8.2 Stage two Establish what the existing data can support 
Redigitize Fainerman modulus and phase independently, use the actual experimental 
conditions, fit the adsorption model or externally constrain it, and compare mobile fast -kinetic, 
slow-kinetic and nuisance alternatives. Expand boundary searches and publish profiles. 
Acceptance gate: the interpretation survives physically reasonable adsorption, diffusion, geometry 
and digitization uncertainty. If it does not, describe the dataset as insufficient for kinetic 
attribution; do not infer a corrected universal capacity. 
8.3 Stage three Obtain decisive external evidence 
Acquire one well-characterized nonionic system with repeated small-amplitude spectra, an 
amplitude sweep and at least one locked validation protocol. Obtain an independent kinetic 
measurement where feasible, with matched conditions and kinetic definitions. A second 
chemically distinct nonionic system would materially strengthen generality. Acceptance gate for a 
positive paper: a physically interpretable kinetic time predicts held -out data and is consistent with 
independent information within combined uncert ainty. Statistical recovery from self -generated 
data cannot substitute for this gate. 
8.4 Stage four Choose the paper from the result 
If kinetics are identified, write a validated methods paper focused on observable design, 
external recovery and uncertainty. If only conditional bounds are identified, write a limits paper 
with explicit domains and escape protocols. If nuisance or equilibrium models explain the data 
equally well, present a model -discrimination study only if it produces a new, practically useful 
conclusion beyond the supplied 2026 work. Avoid splitting unvalidated ionic and Frumkin 
extensions into a second paper before Paper 1 establishes its foundation. 
8.5 Proposed manuscript and figure architecture 
Use Introduction; Physical model and parameter definitions; Experimental data and 
calibration; Analytic limits and numerical verification; Identifiability and protocol design; 
External validation and competing models; Limitations and conclusions. Move development

history to the research record. The abstract should contain the final data scope, validated result, 
uncertainty and explicit boundary of applicability. 
Plan six figures: model hierarchy and state definitions; analytic versus numerical response 
with equilibrium checks; information and profile -likelihood maps; external modulus and phase 
with residuals; locked protocol predictions; amplitude benefit and uncertainty budget. Tables 
should give parameter provenance, model assumptions and publication claims with supporting 
evidence. Do not use a large gallery of one-axis sweeps in place of these decisive tests. 
8.6 Journal positioning and what premium publication would require 
For a theory -led interfacial methods contribution, Langmuir or Journal of Colloid and 
Interface Science could be plausible targets if the scientific gates above are met; this is a scope 
judgment, not an acceptance prediction. A narrower, rigorously verified computational methods 
or instrumentation contribution may fit a specialized venue better. Do not select a prestigious 
target first and inflate the claims to match it. 
The minimum strong package is a correctly defined kinetic parameter, a justified transport 
reduction, an identifiable result under realistic nuisance uncertainty, and external evidence that 
tests the proposed interpretation. An especially strong package adds a validated protocol that 
separates mechanisms existing practice confounds, and demonstrates it on a second system. 
Adding more simulated surfactants, more digits of conditional precision, machine learning, or a 
larger software wrapper will not replace those requirements. 
9. Final assessment 
The project is salvageable, but cosmetic revision is not enough. The original confident 
positive narrative and the later confident negative narrative both outrun the evidence. The highest-
value change is to turn the project into an externally tested inference study with a physically 
derived kinetic time and a consistent equilibrium reservoir. The small -strain analytic result offers 
an efficient route to doing that work. Publication quality will come from resolving the model's 
interpretation and surviving independent prediction, rather than preserving the current headline.
